Byte the Forager

Byte is a hungry robot that can’t read labels. Teach it to tell safe fruit from poison.

Rules and words to know
  1. Each fruit has two features: sweetness (across) and size (up). Safe fruit is a circle, poison is a square.
  2. Byte learns from training fruits (the labelled dots). The shaded map shows what Byte would do anywhere: blue means eat, orange means skip.
  3. Then send Byte into the forest with 25 new fruits it has never seen (the test set). You get 3 trips per level.
  4. Eating safe fruit gives +5 HP, eating poison costs 15 HP, skipping safe fruit costs 3 HP (hunger). Byte starts each trip at 100 HP.
  5. Pass a level by reaching the target accuracy while staying alive. Each level gives Byte a smarter way to learn.

Accuracy = share of fruits judged correctly. Overfitting = memorizing quirks and mistakes in the training data, so new data goes badly. Underfitting = a rule too simple to catch the real pattern.

Welcome! Meet Byte the robot

  1. Teach Byte. Byte learns from the dots on the map. Use the sliders or buttons under the map to change how it learns.
  2. Send Byte out. Press the big button. Byte walks through 25 new fruits and decides to eat or skip each one.
  3. Check the result. Reach the target accuracy without losing all 100 HP to pass the level.

Circle = safe fruit. Square = poison. Blue shading = Byte would eat there. Orange shading = Byte would skip.
